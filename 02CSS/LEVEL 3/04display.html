😎 The old train station stood quietly at the edge of the town, surrounded by tall grass and forgotten trees. Every afternoon, a few birds gathered on the rusty roof while the warm sunlight passed through the broken windows. Nobody seemed to visit the station anymore, except an elderly man who arrived every Sunday carrying a small brown suitcase. He never boarded a train. Instead, he sat on the wooden bench, opened his suitcase, and took out an old collection of photographs. Some showed beautiful mountains, some showed crowded streets, and others showed people whose names had faded from his memory. One evening, a curious boy noticed him and asked why he came there every week. The man smiled and explained that he had once INLINE travelled across many cities from that station, and the photographs reminded him of the people and places he had loved. The boy listened carefully as the sky slowly turned orange. Before leaving, the man handed him one photograph and told him to keep it safe. The next Sunday, when the boy returned, the old man was gone. Only the brown suitcase remained on the bench, along with a short note that simply said, “Every journey leaves something behind.”
